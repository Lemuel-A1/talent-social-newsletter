# Talent Social newsletter kit

Plain-markdown skills that turn a transcript, research report or event notes into an issue of The Talent Social newsletter, in our format, voice and guardrails. Built by VettedAI for the Talent Social community in Nairobi.

**Using it:** see [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md). In short: in any chat, ask the assistant to read [`KIT.md`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Lemuel-A1/talent-social-newsletter/main/KIT.md) (everything in one file) and upload your input.

**What's here:** `ROUTER_PROMPT.md` (start here), `STYLE_GUIDE.md` (voice, anatomy, design, evidence labels, hard rules), `CONFIG.md`, `skills/` (1 brief, 2a–2e drafts by type, 3 subject lines, 4 check, 5 performance log), `templates/`, and `examples/` (Issues 001–003 as sent).

**Forking it for another newsletter:** copy the kit, rewrite section 1 of the style guide and the examples, keep the hard rules unless your audience needs different ones, and start your own lessons log.

This repository is published automatically from a private working repo. Changes made here directly will be overwritten; send suggestions to the Talent Social team.
